The Company Reports Third Quarter Results

Quarterly revenue of $102.5 billion, up 6 percent year over year. Diluted earnings per share of $1.57.

The Company today announced financial results for its fiscal 2026 third quarter ended June 27, 2026. Net income was $27.4 billion for the quarter. The Company generated operating cash flow of $29.8 billion and returned $24.0 billion to shareholders during the quarter.

Services revenue reached an all-time high of $28.9 billion, and the Company expects revenue growth of 4 percent to 6 percent in the next quarter.
